# Grammar and Style Rules for Note Taking

When correcting grammar in the repository's `.md` files, follow these rules:

1. **Spelling & Punctuation:** Correct all spelling errors and punctuation mistakes immediately.
2. **Clarity & Conciseness:** Fix awkward phrasing while maintaining a concise, professional, and technical tone suitable for software architecture notes.
3. **Structure Preservation:** Do not alter the existing note-taking structure (`Goal`, `Lessons`, `Key ideas`, `Takeaway`).
4. **Formatting Consistency:**
   - Maintain established bullet-point and numbering formats.
   - Ensure blank lines separate distinct sections.
5. **Content Integrity:** Do not change the technical meaning or intent of the notes.
6. **Brevity:** Only apply changes that improve readability or correct errors. Do not rewrite for style unless it significantly improves clarity.
